DreamSuite Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: October 20, 2026

DreamSuite is designed local first, so most DreamSuite content lives on your device.

Delete local DreamSuite data

Clear the app data on your device, or uninstall DreamSuite. That removes the DreamSuite content stored on that device.

Revoke Facebook Login access

In Facebook, open Settings and privacy, then Settings, then Apps and websites. Choose DreamSuite, then Remove. This revokes DreamSuite's Facebook Login access for your account.

Request deletion of login information

To request deletion of login-related information received through Facebook Login, email privacy@dreamsuitedreams.com with the subject "DreamSuite data deletion request" and the name used on the Facebook account. We will confirm when the request is complete.
