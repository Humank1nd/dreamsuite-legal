Privacy Policy for DreamSuite

Last updated: October 20, 2026

DreamSuite is a personal AI operating system for VR. It is currently in development and is not public.

Facebook Login

If you sign in with Facebook, Meta sends DreamSuite the basic public profile information allowed by the public_profile permission. DreamSuite uses that information only to recognize the signed-in user inside the app.

Your content

DreamSuite is designed local first. Content you create in DreamSuite is kept on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not use personal information for advertising. We do not share personal information with third parties for marketing.

Data retention

Login information is kept only while it is needed for sign-in and testing. Local app content remains under your control on your device.

Your choices

You can remove DreamSuite from your Facebook Apps and Websites settings to revoke Facebook Login access. You can delete local DreamSuite content by clearing the app data on your device or uninstalling the app.
